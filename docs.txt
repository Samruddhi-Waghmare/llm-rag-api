Nova Cafe is open Monday to Saturday from 8 AM to 9 PM. It is closed on Sundays.

Nova Cafe accepts returns and refunds on unopened packaged items within 14 days of purchase, if you have the receipt. Prepared food and drinks cannot be refunded.

The Nova Cafe loyalty program gives one point for every 10 rupees spent. Collecting 100 points earns a free drink of any size.

Nova Cafe offers vegan, gluten-free, and nut-free options. The menu marks each item with a symbol so customers can identify allergens.

To book the private event room, call the manager at least 7 days ahead. The room holds up to 25 people and costs 3000 rupees for 3 hours.
